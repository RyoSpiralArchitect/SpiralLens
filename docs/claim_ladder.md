# Branched claim taxonomy

Every persisted finding declares the highest level supported by its own
evidence. The taxonomy is branched because nuisance-stable geometry is not
automatically topology.

The canonical interpretation is defined in the
[Order-Parameter-First Fundamental Frame](FUNDAMENTAL_FRAME.md). Frozen
protocols still control individual run thresholds and claim ceilings.
Persisted artifacts still control what was observed. This document cannot
upgrade either one.

## Calibration evidence is not a rung

Analytic or synthetic substrates with a supplied field, charge, core, or
holonomy qualify conditional mathematics and instrument behavior. They do not
promote a real-model claim to Level 1G or Level 1D. A model claim begins at
Level 0 and needs its own substrate-bound observation artifact.

## Level 0 — analogy or unobserved hypothesis

Optical vortex, interference, OAM, phase, core, and topological-defect language
may motivate a hypothesis. A proposed `SupportDiagnostic`, `CoreScore`,
order-parameter construction, graph family, or field estimator also remains
Level 0 until a bound observation artifact exists.

Level 0 language does not establish a model-internal referent.

## Level 1G — geometric observable

A deterministic geometric or transport quantity is defined and reproducibly
computed on a declared substrate, such as:

- an effective-rank, spectral-gap, anisotropy, or coherence
  `SupportDiagnostic`;
- a loop circulation;
- a transport angle;
- a projector or principal-angle field;
- a continuous or relative holonomy;
- an angular spectrum.

Level 1G requires provenance and replay, but it does not require a core or
integer charge. It cannot be relabelled as topological because it is robust or
visually vortex-like.

A support diagnostic may remain at Level 1G permanently. Observation at this
level does not guarantee that a Level 2G promotion path exists.

## Level 1D — defect-field observable

A model-derived order parameter or section is bound to a declared substrate and
reproducibly observed. Its artifact declares:

- field construction and fit scope;
- target and gauge law;
- amplitude or identifiability quantity;
- singular-set/core status, including an explicit unlocalized or undefined
  state;
- interpolation and sampling contract.

A sampled winding may be reported at Level 1D only as the winding of that
declared interpolation. A scalar `CoreScore`, low effective rank, anisotropy,
projected norm, or spectral gap alone is not an order parameter and cannot
enter Level 1D.

## Level 2G — invariant geometric candidate

A Level 1G observable survives all preregistered controls appropriate to its
type:

- basis and declared gauge reparameterization;
- orientation reversal;
- radius and sampling sweeps;
- architecture-factor accounting;
- matched geometric nulls;
- deformation or path controls needed to distinguish curvature from a stable
  transport structure.

Level 2G is a geometric or transport claim. It does not imply Level 2T.

## Level 2T — topological-defect candidate

A Level 1D field additionally satisfies:

- independently defined core or singular-set evidence;
- nonzero amplitude or adequate identifiability on every evaluated boundary;
- branch and sampling resolution;
- a charge interpretation justified by the declared target space;
- nested, reverse, off-core, and deformation controls;
- matched cycle or homology class across every required graph-construction
  family;
- the full crossed field-estimation-graph by cycle-graph null;
- worst-case `pass` with no required family removed after observation.

An evaluable disagreement is `fail_graph_dependence`. An unmatched class,
unresolved field, non-orientable bundle, or inadequate graph support is
`insufficient`, never an automatic pass.

Level 2T is still a model-internal structural claim. It is not semantics, a
physical optical vortex, or OAM.

## Level 3 — semantic and causal

A Level 2G or Level 2T candidate predicts held-out semantic contrasts and a
norm-preserving intervention changes the relevant downstream behavior
selectively under matched sham controls.

Only Level 3 supports a semantic and causal interpretation. An SAE label,
decoded string, or post-hoc explanation does not raise an earlier artifact's
level.

## Promotion rules

- Promotion is per artifact or candidate, never inherited from a run.
- A ledger records a promotion decision but cannot supply missing evidence.
- `pass`, `fail`, `insufficient`, and `not_run` remain distinct.
- A required `insufficient` or `not_run` gate blocks promotion.
- A Level 2G result cannot be cited as Level 2T.
- Geometry and defect branches may later be joined only by an explicit,
  same-substrate binding.
- Historical artifacts retain their original bytes and labels; the
  [Interpretation Ledger](EXPERIMENT_INTERPRETATION_LEDGER.md) records their
  current authorized reading without rewriting them.
