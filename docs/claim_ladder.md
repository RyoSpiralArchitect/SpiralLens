# Claim ladder

Every persisted finding declares the highest level supported by its evidence.

## Level 0 — analogy

Optical vortex, interference, and OAM language motivates a hypothesis. No
model-internal observable has yet been established.

## Level 1 — observable

A deterministic quantity is defined and reproducibly computed, such as a loop
circulation, transport angle, angular spectrum, or sampled-winding estimate.

## Level 2 — invariant candidate

The observable survives its preregistered controls: basis reparameterization,
orientation reversal, radius and sampling sweeps, architecture-factor
accounting, and matched nulls.

## Level 3 — semantic and causal

The invariant candidate predicts held-out semantic contrasts and a
norm-preserving intervention changes the relevant downstream behavior
selectively.

The current SpiralLens v0.1 executable path emits Level-1 candidates only.
Level 2 is the next gated target and requires the candidate-to-loop and null
runner described in the roadmap. Semantic labels and SAE annotations may be
attached later, but they do not retroactively change the discovery record.
