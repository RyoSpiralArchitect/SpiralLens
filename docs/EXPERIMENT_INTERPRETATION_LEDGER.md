This ledger rereads the experiment system that existed at the historical
snapshot under the order-parameter-first frame. The reframe is a post-outcome
change to the research question, not a reinterpretation of what the audit
generated from that snapshot measured. It does not change what happened,
rewrite a protocol, upgrade a result, or pretend that the new frame governed
an older one-shot.

For every entry, two statements remain visible:

1. what the original contract asked and observed;
2. what that evidence can mean under the fundamental frame.

The second statement may reduce or partition a claim. It cannot increase the
evidence carried by the original artifact.

## 1. Temporal integrity rules

- Earlier Git commits, protocol bytes, freeze bytes, receipts, audit outputs,
  and reservation markers remain immutable.
- The fundamental frame begins at the commit that introduces it. The frame is
  not backdated.
- A historical result may be reclassified but not relabelled inside its frozen

  rerun under the same identity.
- The Pythia-70M outcomes below cannot select Pythia-160M confirmatory fields,
  graph families, thresholds, contexts, or exclusions.

  canonical repository history. A cumulative integration carrying such a

## 3. Artifact-by-artifact reading

### 3.1 Analytic rotation and winding phantoms

**Original purpose**

Qualify numerical loop, holonomy, winding, reverse-orientation, nested-radius,
off-core, pure-gauge, and sampling-alias behavior on analytic fields whose
ground truth is supplied by construction.

**Evidence retained**

- the continuous-holonomy and sampled-winding implementations are separate;
- winding of a supplied complex field respects amplitude, branch, and integer
  residual gates;
- coarse sampling can alias a higher supplied charge;
- pure-gauge, stretch, shear, and off-core controls behave as declared.

**Fundamental reading**

These tests qualify mathematical primitives conditional on a supplied field.
They do not identify a transformer substrate, define a model-derived order
parameter, localize a Pythia core, or establish a topology claim about a model.

**Forbidden rereading**

An analytic vortex positive cannot serve as evidence that the same
order-parameter referent exists in Pythia.

### 3.2 ContextBank and activation atlas

**Original purpose**

Bind model revision, context, roles, positions, sweep domain, tokenizer
fingerprint, row order, and state/update arrays into a replayable observation
artifact while excluding semantic labels from discovery.

**Evidence retained**

The atlas is a valid candidate substrate source. ContextBank identities and
roles prevent several forms of context and semantic leakage.

**Fundamental reading**

An atlas supplies observations on which a future field may be defined. It is
not itself a phase field, language-space atlas, semantic atlas, manifold
certificate, or order parameter.

**Required future join**

Any `SubstrateBinding` must bind the exact atlas, row universe, comparison
group, state/update tensors, fit scope, metric, and field-estimation
neighborhood.

**Public-example engineering subtype**

An atlas carrying a frozen `public_example_engineering` binding is narrower
than the general atlas contract. It proves only that an exact cached model
revision could be loaded, bounded residual observations could be persisted,
and the journal, array checksums, manifest, and receipt could be reloaded.
The access receipt must say `model_accessed=true` and
`activation_values_persisted=true`; those facts do not make the run
claim-eligible. Its only allowed consumer is atlas integrity validation.

**Forbidden rereading**

The public-example engineering atlas cannot be used to emit candidates or a
neighbor audit, construct a model-bound `SubstrateBinding`, run F0-F4, select a
layer or context, or support graph, field, core, loop, phase, holonomy,
winding, semantic, SAE, causal, or integer claims.

### 3.3 High-cosine and divergent-update candidate rule

**Original purpose**

Retrieve state-near pairs and exactly gate their norm and update divergence
without using decoded strings, SAE labels, or projected coordinates.

**Evidence retained**

The rule remains a semantics-free structural candidate generator and a useful
way to identify locally surprising response differences.

**Fundamental reading**

A pair is not a loop, a field, a core, a phase discontinuity, or a topological
defect. It may seed later substrate or field analysis only through a separate
protocol.

**Forbidden rereading**

Large drift divergence cannot be used as \(|\psi|\), phase shift, or defect
charge without an order-parameter construction.

### 3.4 Exact and approximate neighbor backends

**Original purpose**

Use state-only retrieval to propose canonical row pairs, then recompute every
candidate gate from original atlas values in float64. Bind approximate
retrieval to exact-reference audits and receipts.

**Evidence retained**

The backend boundary, exact reranking, canonical pair identity, runtime
binding, and fail-closed persistence remain valid engineering foundations.

**Fundamental reading**

Neighbor retrieval controls computational coverage. It does not define the
scientific graph family used for field estimation, cycle construction, or
topology. `neighbors/` therefore remains upstream and separate from future
`graphs/` and order-parameter contracts.

### 3.5 Frozen recall-gate methodology

**Original purpose**

Require aggregate, query-local, relative-density, cosine-boundary, joint-cell,
worst-case, and cold-rebuild support before approximate proposals can be
promoted.

**Evidence retained**

Zero denominators and under-supported cells are `insufficient`, never recall
1.0. Averages cannot hide a local retrieval collapse.

**Fundamental reading**

This is a retrieval-coverage methodology. Its graph terminology does not
provide graph-construction-family invariance, homology matching, or a topology
gate.

`fail`, `insufficient`, and `not_run` never mean absence. Co-occurrence is
descriptive and cannot select later instrumentation or hypotheses.

**Transition and claim boundary**

The design separates an instrument detection boundary, an operational
phase-like-regime outcome, and a model transition.
Synthetic injection amplitude, noise, sampling/local density, dipole
separation, graph, and architecture nuisances first define where a later null
could be qualified. A sharp boundary on that surface is not a model phase
transition.

The primary future model-transition axis is ordered training checkpoint.
Layer remains architectural depth, not time; intervention strength is reserved
as a later causal-probe axis. Exact checkpoint hashes/training steps, a common
model/tokenizer/context/address contract, and common intervention coordinates
must be sealed first. Comparisons use gauge-invariant quantities or alignment
fit only on the fit partition; outcome-selected basis/alignment is forbidden,
and optimizer/data-mixture/schedule discontinuities are declared covariates.
Discovery may fit one bounded change-point window, but hidden confirmation
cannot move it. A transition candidate requires a held-out change in at least
one field/core observable and one independently computed geometry or defect
observable, overlapping change-point uncertainty, required graph robustness,
adequate sensitivity, replication across required context strata, and complete
required strata. Single spikes, coverage loss, smooth drift, or post-outcome
hypothesis/graph/layer/alignment/checkpoint selection do not qualify.

This is a design record, not a protocol or source freeze. It constructs no F2
or F4 model field, order parameter, core, holonomy, winding, phase-like regime,
transition, or qualified null. All execution, model, network, raw-capture,
subject, Pythia-70M, and Pythia-160M authorities are false. Scientific,
topology, integer, semantic, publication, VOY, D7/D8, SCI-S1/S2, API, support,
compatibility, portability, release, and library states remain unchanged. The
next safe implementation candidate is model-free graph-scale transport and
multi-nuisance selector plumbing under a separate dated decision; no v0.3
freeze or launch is authorized here.

## 5. Ledger change control

Future entries append a dated interpretation event with its source commit,
decision timing, affected artifacts, and claim delta. Corrections preserve the
superseded text and identify the replacement; they do not silently edit an
earlier observation into agreement with the current frame.

An entry may narrow or partition an authorized interpretation. It cannot
change frozen bytes, replace an artifact status, or promote evidence beyond
the protocol and observation that produced it.

## 6. Forward rule

reuse an old artifact label as evidence for a new scientific object.
